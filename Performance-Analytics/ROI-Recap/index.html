Why this exists

Buyer Intent customers churn because they don't feel ROI from the product. The existing Performance Analytics page is the previous team's attempt — it isn't used often. Three root causes Jeremy validated:

1. Attribution disbelief — "deals influenced by G2" math isn't trusted.
2. CRM-integration gate — customers churn before ever reaching the ROI page.
3. CSMs / day-to-day users aren't feeling it — page isn't surfaced in renewals or workflow.

The reframe: shift the story from "deals we closed for you" to "buyers we surfaced, who they match, and who else is in the race." Sidesteps attribution disbelief by reporting on what's verifiable (signal volume, ICP match, competitive overlap) rather than what's contested (causation in closed deals). Works whether or not the customer has connected a CRM.

What's deliberately not in v1: "What your team did in G2" (buyer report views, contacts downloaded, etc.) was originally proposed as the strongest counter to attribution disbelief — but a data audit confirmed G2 doesn't currently instrument vendor-admin actions. Building that data layer is a separate ~1-2 week instrumentation project; cut from the prototype to avoid promising data we don't have.
